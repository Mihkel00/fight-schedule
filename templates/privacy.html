What We Collect

Fight Schedule uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. We collect:

  • Page views and navigation patterns
  • Device type and browser information
  • Geographic location (city/country level only)
  • Referral sources

We do NOT collect personal information like names, emails, or IP addresses.

Cookies

Google Analytics cookies are only set if you choose Accept in the cookie bar. If you decline, or don't answer, Google Analytics is not loaded at all.

  • Google Analytics (only with your consent): anonymous traffic analysis
  • Your cookie choice: stored in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again

Fight times are converted to your timezone in your browser; nothing is stored for that.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics processes data on our behalf to provide insights about site usage. Google may use this data in accordance with their own privacy policy. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy.

Usage Counts

To see which parts of the site are useful we count page views and taps on elements (a fight card, a filter, a "more fights" button, a calendar or watch link). A page also counts as "seen" once it has been on screen for five seconds, which tells people apart from automated visitors. Each count records only the kind of page, the element, the time, and whether the browser looks automated. No cookie is set and no IP address, user ID or other personal data is stored, so these counts cannot be linked to you.

Affiliate Links

Some "Watch on" links to streaming services are affiliate links. If you subscribe through one of them we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. This never affects which fights we list or which service we show — we display the broadcaster that actually carries the event. Clicks on these links are counted (provider, event, page, browser type and browser language, to tell people from automated crawlers) so we can see what is useful; no IP address or other personal data is stored.

Third-Party Services

We display fighter images and fight data from public sources. We do not share your data with any third parties beyond Google Analytics.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Change your cookie choice at any time: (declining removes Google Analytics cookies)
  • Block cookies in your browser settings
  • Opt out of Google Analytics via browser add-on
  • Use private/incognito browsing mode

Data Retention

Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months, after which it is automatically deleted.

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us via our main site.
